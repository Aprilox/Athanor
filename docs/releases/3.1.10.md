## What's new in 3.1.10

- **A project's Chat follows Claude's answer by itself**: while Claude answers (a sub-agent it proposes included), the thread now refreshes on its own and the answer appears as soon as it ends, without leaving the tab and coming back.

## Nouveautés de la 3.1.10
